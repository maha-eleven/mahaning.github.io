01 / About

Academic work with a practical lens.

I work across teaching, research, and professional practice in Information Systems. I’m particularly interested in how organizations adopt enterprise technologies, improve processes, and translate digital capabilities into useful outcomes.

At BINUS University’s School of Information Systems, my teaching and academic work span Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Information Systems Analysis and Design, Business Process Management, Knowledge Management, and Robotic Process Automation.

My professional experience also includes enterprise resource planning consulting at Accenture, curriculum and learning-content development, and training in digital and business technologies.

I hold a Master of Commerce, specializing in Enterprise System and Business Design, from UNSW, supported by an LPDP scholarship, and an undergraduate degree in Business Management of Telecommunication and Informatics from Telkom University.

02 / Research

Questions that connect systems and people.

My research interests sit at the intersection of digital technologies, business processes, and learning practices. I’m interested in the implementation of technology—not only what a system can do, but how it is adopted, taught, governed, and evaluated.

Research area 01

ERP & Enterprise Architecture

Enterprise system adoption, ERP education, cloud adoption strategy, and integration across business functions.

Research area 02

Automation & Process Improvement

Robotic process automation, business process management, and evidence-based evaluation of digital workflows.

Research area 03

Governance & Digital Capability

IT governance, digital transformation, knowledge management, and practical approaches for organizational contexts.
